Rules

  • Watch, Island and Smart Stack are always ink-0. The phone follows the system appearance.
  • Signal colours fill only status: dots, rings, chips, banners. Text on a signal fill is ink-0.
  • Hollow means unconfirmed. A shot the camera never acknowledged is drawn as an outline and labelled “sent”, never “taken”.
  • No gradients, no brand-maker colours. Camera brands appear as text badges.
